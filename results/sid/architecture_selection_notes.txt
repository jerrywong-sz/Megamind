SID-B BASIC ROBUSTNESS ARCHITECTURE ANALYSIS

Models
------
EfficientNet-B0 SID-B
ConvNeXt-Tiny SID-B
DINOv2 ViT-S/14 SID-B

Evaluation
----------
Dataset: SID validation
Images per condition: 5,099
Conditions: clean + 15 isolated robustness damages
Threshold: 0.5
Seed: 42

Main Findings
-------------
1. ConvNeXt-Tiny obtained the highest numerical SID
   robustness result.

2. EfficientNet-B0 was extremely close to ConvNeXt.

3. Paired EffNet vs ConvNeXt comparison:
   - ConvNeXt-only correct: 170
   - EffNet-only correct: 134
   - Both wrong: 46
   - ConvNeXt won 12/16 conditions
   - EffNet won 2/16
   - 2/16 tied
   - No individual condition had exact McNemar p < 0.05.

4. Mean measured inference latency:
   - EfficientNet-B0: approximately 1.14 ms/image
   - ConvNeXt-Tiny: approximately 4.12 ms/image
   - DINOv2: approximately 4.34 ms/image

5. DINOv2 was substantially weaker, particularly:
   - colour +0.20: ~92.14% accuracy
   - crop 0.80: ~85.23% accuracy

Current Interpretation
----------------------
Raw SID robustness winner:
ConvNeXt-Tiny

Best current efficiency/performance trade-off:
EfficientNet-B0

DINOv2:
Remove from primary architecture shortlist.

ConvNeXt should not yet be declared the final architecture.
Its robustness advantage over EfficientNet is small relative
to its substantially higher inference cost.

The next decisive comparison should focus on generalisation /
cross-domain performance after the model-selection protocol
is agreed.
